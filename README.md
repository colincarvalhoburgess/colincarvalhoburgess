# Colin Carvalho Burgess

**Audio Engineering · AI Voice · Creative Audio · Interactive Storytelling**

I'm an audio director, producer, editor and recording engineer with 20+ years' professional experience across the BBC, Audible/Amazon, immersive audio and interactive storytelling.

I'm currently building **[Whisperworld](https://audioworld.app/world)** — a voice-navigated spatial storytelling platform combining ElevenLabs-generated characters, recorded dialogue, spatial audio, voice interaction and branching narrative.

My work sits at the intersection of professional audio craft and emerging technology, particularly:

- AI voice and generative audio
- Dialogue editing and audio post-production
- Creative sound design
- Spatial and immersive audio
- Voice and performance direction
- Interactive and branching narrative
- Creative tools for audio storytellers
